# Plan: V2 3.4 near-expiry and 3.6 Schedule H1

Owner decisions (2026-10-17): both are **flags, never a review hold** - shown on the line and
summed up on the bill, approval needs no override. Near-expiry uses a workspace window
(default 6 months, 0 turns it off). Schedule H1 uses the official molecule list, plus products
a reviewer marks as H1 once, remembered for the workspace (bills print brand names).

Two PRs, 3.6 stacked on 3.4, each with its web PR.

## 3.4 Near-expiry at receipt

### Why

A distributor that ships stock with a few months left leaves the chemist with stock they
cannot sell. Today `line.shelf_life` warns at a fixed 3 months; each business has its own
line (many return anything under 6 months).

### What

- `workspace_settings` (migration 0041): one row per workspace, `near_expiry_months`
  (0-36, default 6 when there is no row), who changed it and when. Row-level security like
  every workspace table; erased with the account.
- `line.shelf_life` version 2: a warning on each line that expires less than the window after
  the bill date, "Stock expires 2027-02, 4 months after the bill date; this workspace flags
  stock with less than 6 months." Not run when the window is 0. The expired check
  (`line.not_expired`, an error) is unchanged.
- The rule leaves the fixed rule set and becomes an extra rule built from the window:
  - when a bill is read (the worker reads the window with the version), so the stored
    assessment and `/assessment` have it;
  - on every review read, with the window as it is now, so a change applies to bills still
    being reviewed.
- `GET /v1/settings` (any signed-in role) and `PATCH /v1/settings` (an administrator, or a
  personal workspace's own person): `{near_expiry_months}`; a change is audited
  (`settings.changed`, old and new value).
- `ReviewOut.near_expiry_months`, so the screen can say what the window is.

### Web

- A failed warning on a value reads as "Near expiry" (amber), not "Failed" (red).
- The values tab sums it up above the lines: "2 lines expire within 6 months of the bill".
- Account page: a "Workspace settings" card with the window, for whoever may change it.

### Tests (first)

The window as set, default 6, 0 off, the boundary month, a bill without a date (not
evaluated), only a warning (decision unchanged), a change applied to a bill in review,
the stored assessment from the worker, the settings API (roles, limits, audit), erasure.

## 3.6 Schedule H1 on the purchase bill

### Why

A chemist must enter every Schedule H1 drug sold in a separate register, kept three years
(Drugs Rules, 1945, as amended by G.S.R. 588(E)). Knowing at receipt which lines are H1 means they are shelved and sold that way.

### What counts as H1

1. **The molecule list** (`docforge/trust/schedule_h1.py`), each entry with the notification
   that added it and its date in force, matched as a whole word in the product name:
   - 46 from G.S.R. 588(E), 30 Aug 2013, in force 1 Mar 2014 (the gazette's "Donpenem" is
     Doripenem);
   - oxytocin, in force 1 Sep 2018; tapentadol, G.S.R. 258(E), in force 1 Nov 2021;
     oseltamivir and zanamivir, in force 5 Feb 2024;
   - pregabalin, G.S.R. 377(E) of 13 May 2026, in force 16 Nov 2026 (180 days after its
     publication on 20 May). A bill dated earlier says it joins H1 on that date.
   - Draft G.S.R. 745(E) of 19 Aug 2026 (flupentixol, zopiclone, gabapentin, carisoprodol)
     is not final and not included.
2. **Marked products**: a reviewer marks a line's product as H1; every later line with the
   same product name (case, spaces and punctuation aside) in that workspace is H1. A mark can
   be removed. `h1_products` (migration 0042): workspace, key, name as marked, who, when;
   row-level security; marking and removing are audited; erased with the account.

### Behaviour

- `ReviewOut.schedule_h1`: one entry per H1 line - `{path, product_name, molecule,
  product_id, in_force_from}` (molecule for a list match, product_id for a marked product).
- `GET/POST /v1/schedule-h1/products`, `DELETE /v1/schedule-h1/products/{id}`: reading for
  any role, marking and removing for reviewers.
- Not a blocker and not a rule result: H1 is neither pass nor fail.

### Web

A "Schedule H1" badge on the line, "1 Schedule H1 line - enter it in the H1 register" above
the lines, and on each line "Mark as Schedule H1" (or "Remove H1 mark" for a marked product).

### Tests (first)

Each molecule's word match (not inside another word), a combination product, a marked
product found on a later bill with other spacing and case, a removed mark, another
workspace's mark never seen, pregabalin before and after its date, the API's roles and audit.
